# 0087 — Cards you may still play from your piles

**Status:** Approved 2026-11-13 (owner, by prompt, on the canvas "gaps-marks-1113" v2, sections 6-9, redrawn on the real plate look, https://claude.ai/artifact/7HeVgwnuurE3PhCFiqvdxa)
**Owner decision:** **A · glow and a fan beside the plate**.

Phase 10 A-22, from the deep dive "Phase 10 graphical gaps.md" (2026-11-13), item 3.

## Why

The exile and graveyard tiles show only a count (`GameScreen.tsx` ~2750-2763). "You may play it", time counters and "Exiled by" appear only inside the opened tray or the close-up (`zoneTray.ts` ~12-35, `GameScreen.tsx` ~4150; worker `ViewerSnapshot.java` ~336-342 already sends playable). A suspended card's time counter coming off each upkeep is invisible.

## Built

1. **Glow.** When a player has cards they may play from their graveyard or exile, those tiles glow gold. This covers adventure, foretell, Light Up the Stage-style "until end of turn", flashback, escape, suspend, and cards the game marks playable from there.
2. **Fan beside the plate.**
   - A small fan of those cards stands on the table just left of the plate, level with the Graveyard and Exile tiles. For your own plate that is the bottom-right corner of the table; for the other player's, the top-right corner.
   - Each card rises out of its tile into the fan when it becomes playable. Each wears its pile's corner sign (graveyard or exile).
   - It leaves the fan (back into its tile) when it stops being playable.
   - You cast straight from the fan, with the same click and rise as from the hand (0072/0081). The fan closes the gap.
3. **Suspend.** A suspended card stands in the fan, dimmed (not castable), with a round time badge. Each upkeep the badge ticks with a rising −1. At zero it is cast for free and rises from the fan.
4. **Hidden information.** The other player sees only what is public. A foretold card is a face-down back in their view of your fan, with no name. A card exiled face down and unknown to them is never named.
5. A click hurries everything (A-00). Reduce motion: no movement; the glow, fan and badge still show. Nothing covers the hand, the plate or the act button. Real-screen placement as on the canvas (1920x1080 and above, UI-006).

## Amendments

### Amendment 1 — widened before building (owner, 2026-11-13, from "Phase 10 deep dive 2.md")

Approved looks only; no new canvas needed.
- **Use the game's complete list.** Today the worker marks only exile cards under a "you may play it" effect. Cards in the graveyard (flashback, escape, Faithless Looting, Think Twice, Uro) and foretold, plotted and suspended cards are never marked, so this spec's own tests would have nothing to light. Build the glow and fan from the game's own complete list of cards a player can use outside their hand, never from card names.
- **Abilities from the graveyard** (Unearth, Scavenge, Embalm, Encore, Agent of Kotis's Renew) stand in the fan too, with the ability's own small mark. Using one rises from the fan as a cast does.

- **Plotted and foretold cards** wear their gold-rimmed square in the fan (plotted: a clock-hand; foretold: an eye; face down to the other player as before).

### Amendment 3 — no Your move window for pile cards; the fan card is the cue (owner, 2026-11-15)

Decided on the canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/Whuv712DyP5u7StikaGGoj, option **A: "no window, the glowing card is the cue"**. A 0087 defect, found from the owner's live report: on your move, a card you could cast from your graveyard or exile (Think Twice's flashback) opened the "Your move" window, and a click on its fan card did nothing.

- **Why.** The game screen sorts each option of your move by whether the board draws the card it names (battlefield, hand, stack, players, 0088's library top); every other option is listed, and a list opens the window. The fan's cards were never counted as drawn. So the window opened, the fan card was never marked choosable (no breath), and a click on it found no options. The As built line "A click on a fan card is a click on that card" was untrue until this amendment; its page test handed the board the card's clickable state by hand (now forbidden, AGENTS TEST-003).
- **Built.** The cards the fan draws count as drawn (`GameScreen.tsx`, the `visible` set: `fanCards(board)`). On your move no window opens for them; the Graveyard or Exile tile glows (as built); the fan card breathes gold like a playable hand card (0090's Breath layer), or is dimmed and does not breathe when it cannot be paid for now (§7.54, as a hand card); a click on it casts it, exactly like a hand card. "List choices" still lists it. An option for anything the fan does not draw (an emblem, a companion, a card that may only be looked at) is still listed and still opens the window, so nothing becomes unreachable.
